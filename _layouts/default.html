Thijmen Nijdam

I am a Robot Learning Research Engineer at the University of Amsterdam (VISLab), working with Toyota Motor Europe and the Toyota Research Institute on real-to-sim-to-real robot learning for manipulation.

ScholarMailLinkedInGitHubCV

I did my BSc and MSc in AI at the University of Amsterdam, where I also worked as a research intern on video generation, information retrieval and uncertainty quantification. My MSc thesis on local world models was supervised by Dr. Andrii Zadaianchuk and Prof. Efstratios Gavves.

Next to my studies, I worked for four years as a freelance AI developer at LeerLevels, an ed-tech startup, building LLM-based applications for teachers and students.

Outside of work, I enjoy hiking, running, playing squash and chess, and nerding out over Formula 1.
