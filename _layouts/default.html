About Me

I am a Robot Learning Research Engineer at the University of Amsterdam, working with the VISLab under the supervision of Prof. Efstratios Gavves and in collaboration with Toyota Motor Europe and the Toyota Research Institute. I work on real-to-sim-to-real robot learning for manipulation on a Franka arm.

I did my BSc and MSc in AI at the University of Amsterdam, where I worked as a research intern on projects in information retrieval, uncertainty quantification, and benchmarking physical reasoning in video generation. My MSc thesis on local world models for articulated object manipulation, supervised by Dr. Andrii Zadaianchuk and Prof. Efstratios Gavves, sparked my interest in robot learning and world models, and was published at the Robot World Models Workshop at RSS 2026.

Alongside my main project, I co-supervise an MSc research project extending the Morpheus benchmark for physical reasoning in video generation models.

Outside of work, I enjoy hiking, running, playing squash and chess, and nerding out over Formula 1.
