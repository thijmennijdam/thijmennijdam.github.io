Thijmen Nijdam

I am a Robot Learning Research Engineer at the University of Amsterdam (VISLab), working with Toyota Motor Europe and the Toyota Research Institute on real-to-sim-to-real robot learning for manipulation.

ScholarMailLinkedInGitHubCV

I did my BSc and MSc in AI at the University of Amsterdam. My MSc thesis on local world models, supervised by Dr. Andrii Zadaianchuk and Prof. Efstratios Gavves, was published at the Robot World Models Workshop at RSS 2026.

Outside of work, I enjoy hiking, running, playing squash and chess, and nerding out over Formula 1.
