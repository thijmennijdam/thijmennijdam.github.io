European Robotics Forum Hackathon

Rotterdam, 2022

In this hackathon, university teams programmed robots for tasks from farming. Our University of Amsterdam team worked with two Lely Juno robots, which push feed back towards the cows in a barn, and made them drive through a simulated barn on their own.

What we built

  • Two ways to navigate. One robot followed the walls using sonar. The other built a map with a LiDAR and drove along waypoints.
  • Sharing narrow passages. The robots told each other over ROS when a passage was occupied, so one could wait.
  • Finding home. A camera looked for a colored marker at the base station, and the robot steered towards it.
